# E1007 — Gas calculation overflow

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Code** | `1007` |
| **Slug** | `E1007` |
| **Category** | `gas` (Gas) |
| **CIP** | CIP-3 |

## What happened

Gas calculation overflow.

## Why it happened

The product of fee × gas_limit overflows a u128 integer.

## How to fix

Lower fee or `gas_limit` values so their product fits in u128.
