# E1411 — Transfer hook exceeded gas cap

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Code** | `1411` |
| **Slug** | `E1411` |
| **Category** | `gas` (Gas) |
| **CIP** | CIP-20 §8.3 |

## What happened

Transfer hook exceeded gas cap.

## Why it happened

The transfer hook consumed more than the 50,000-cycle cap defined in CIP-20 §8.3.

## How to fix

Simplify hook logic to stay under the 50,000-cycle cap.
